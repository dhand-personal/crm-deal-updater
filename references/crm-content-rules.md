# What never goes into a CRM record

CRM records are read by colleagues, managers, other teams, and future
owners of the account. They can be exported, shared with partners, and
requested in legal discovery. Run every proposed value through this
list before the review (SKILL.md Step 8).

## Hold back entirely

- **Opinions about individuals.** "She's difficult," "he's not very
  sharp," "seems checked out." Record the observable fact instead, if it
  matters: "Asked for the pricing to be re-sent twice; hasn't replied
  since 9/12."
- **Personal details.** Health, family, pregnancy, religion, politics,
  personal finances, relationships, job-search rumors about a person.
  Even if the customer volunteered it on a call.
- **Anything said off the record** or with "don't write this down,"
  "between us," or similar.
- **Unverified claims about competitors.** "Competitor X is going out of
  business," "their product is broken." Record what the customer said
  about the competitor, attributed: "Customer said Competitor X's
  pricing came in about 20% lower."
- **Credentials, account numbers, or other sensitive identifiers** that
  show up in an email or transcript.

## Rewrite before proposing

- **Internal jargon or harsh shorthand** from internal threads ("this
  deal is a dog") becomes neutral: "Internal view: low confidence in
  Q4 close."
- **Speculation** becomes attributed: not "budget is probably $200k"
  but "Rep estimate: budget around $200k (not confirmed by customer)."
- **Long quotes** get trimmed to the phrase that carries the point.
- **Conclusions the skill drew** become the facts behind them. Record
  what was said, not what it implies: not "no champion after 10/9" but
  "Marcus leaves 10/9; Kevin Osei taking over, not yet briefed." Put the
  implication in the review for the rep (the stage section or Gaps),
  not in the field.

## Tell the rep

For each item held back or rewritten, one line in the review's "Held
back" section: what it was about (not the content itself, if it's
personal) and why. Example: "Held back a personal detail about the
champion from the 9/18 call."

The rep can override a rewrite by editing the text, but the skill
doesn't write held-back personal details even if asked. It explains
why, and the rep can enter it in the CRM themselves if they still want
it there.
