# Methodologies: elements, signals, and typical CRM labels

Used in setup (SKILL.md, "First run") to recognize the methodology from
the CRM's own field labels and to propose the field map. Also used each
run to decide what evidence to look for.

Field labels vary a lot between companies. The "typical labels" below
are starting points for matching, not a required list. Match on the
label and the field description together, and always confirm with the
rep. Never map a field on name similarity alone when its description
says otherwise.

Signal strength from labels, per methodology:
- **Strong:** 3 or more elements have a clearly matching field.
- **Partial:** 1 or 2.
- **None.**

Blends are normal. Command of the Message plus MEDDPICC is common.

If nothing scores and the rep can't name a methodology, the default is
MEDDPICC (SKILL.md setup step 5). Tell the rep it's a default they can
change.
Report every methodology that scores partial or better, and ask the rep
to confirm.

## MEDDPICC / MEDDIC

| Element | What to capture | Typical labels |
|---|---|---|
| Metrics | Quantified outcome the customer expects, and how they'll measure it | Metrics, Success Metrics, Quantified Value |
| Economic Buyer | Who controls the budget; their engagement and stance | Economic Buyer, EB, Budget Owner |
| Decision Criteria | What they'll judge options on, technical and business | Decision Criteria, Evaluation Criteria |
| Decision Process | Steps, people, and timeline to a decision | Decision Process, Buying Process |
| Paper Process | Legal, security, procurement, and contracting steps | Paper Process, Procurement Process, Legal Process |
| Identify Pain | The business pain driving the evaluation | Pain, Identified Pain, Business Pain |
| Champion | Internal advocate with influence, and the evidence they're advocating | Champion, Internal Champion, Coach |
| Competition | Other vendors, internal build, or doing nothing | Competition, Competitors, Competitive Landscape |

Signals in communications: a named budget holder separate from the
day-to-day contact; a quantified outcome; an internal advocate acting on
their own initiative; procurement or security steps named; competitors
named.

## Command of the Message

| Element | What to capture | Typical labels |
|---|---|---|
| Before Scenario | Current state in the customer's words | Before Scenario, Current State |
| Negative Consequences | Cost of staying in the current state | Negative Consequences, Pain, Cost of Inaction |
| After Scenario | Future state the customer describes | After Scenario, Future State |
| Positive Business Outcomes | Measurable results they want | PBOs, Positive Business Outcomes |
| Required Capabilities | Must-haves that decide the evaluation | Required Capabilities, RCs, Must-Haves |
| Metrics | How success will be measured | Metrics |
| Differentiation / How We Do It | Why this vendor meets the required capabilities | Differentiators, How We Do It |

Command of the Message often sits on top of MEDDPICC fields. Treat "CoM
plus MEDDPICC" as the default expectation when both score.

## SPIN Selling

Situation, Problem, Implication, Need-Payoff. Rarely stored as CRM
fields. More often it shows in discovery notes.

## Challenger

Commercial Insight, Reframe, Tailored Value, Constructive Tension. Rarely
stored as CRM fields.

## CHAMP

Challenges, Authority, Money, Prioritization.

## NEAT Selling

Need, Economic Impact, Access to Authority, Timeline.

## VALUE Selling

Value Hypothesis, Quantified Value, Validation.

## SNAP Selling

Simple, iNvaluable, Aligned, Priority.

## Solution Selling

Pain Chain, Vision, Value Justification, Access to Power.

## Fields every methodology benefits from

Whatever the methodology, these standard or near-standard fields are
worth mapping if they exist, because they feed stage checks:

- Next Step
- Compelling Event or Critical Event (a dated reason to act)
- Close Date and Amount (Replace only)

## Starter exit criteria by stage type

When the CRM gives no exit criteria (the usual case), setup proposes
these, adjusted to the methodology in play and marked "proposed." The
rep edits them. Stage names vary; match on the stage's position and
purpose.

| Stage type | Proposed exit criteria |
|---|---|
| Discovery / Qualification | Pain confirmed in the customer's words; a quantified impact or clear cost of inaction; next meeting agreed |
| Solution / Evaluation | Decision criteria and required capabilities known; economic buyer identified; champion identified; competition known |
| Proposal / Business Case | Metrics agreed with the customer; economic buyer engaged; decision process mapped with dates |
| Negotiation / Commit | Paper process mapped; pricing reviewed with the economic buyer; target signature date agreed |

These are starting points only. Every company's version is different,
and the rep's confirmed version always wins.
